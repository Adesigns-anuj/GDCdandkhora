About the College

Rooted in Dandkhora, built for first-generation learners.

Rajkiya Degree Mahavidyalaya, Dandkhora opened in 2026 to bring affordable, quality higher education within reach of students across the Dandkhora block of Katihar district — many of whom are the first in their families to attend college. Our founding batch of 128 students is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts on a green, tree-lined campus built to grow with them.

As the Government of Bihar fills the college's sanctioned teaching posts — twelve new faculty members are expected to join our founding team of two — we plan to expand into Science and Commerce, giving Dandkhora's students even more reasons to stay and study close to home.

From the Principal's Desk

A message to our first batch

It is with great pride that I welcome the very first batch of Rajkiya Degree Mahavidyalaya, Dandkhora — 128 students who have placed their trust in a college that is, quite literally, being built around them. We are young — two teachers today, fourteen soon; one programme today, three planned — but what we lack in scale, we make up for in attention. Every student here is known, not just enrolled.

I look forward to standing with each of you at Convocation in 2030, and to Dandkhora carrying your name a little further than it has travelled before.

Prof. Vinoy Kr. Pandey
Principal, GDC Dandkhora
